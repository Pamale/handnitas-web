Requirements

To quote on Handnitas you must be registered as a professional or business and comply with the regulations applicable to you where you provide the service.

If you also provide your registration documents and your public liability insurance, you earn the Verified badge, visible to every client.

Handnitas checks that the documents exist and are in force. It does not supervise or guarantee the work carried out.
